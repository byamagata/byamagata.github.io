I've finally started this blog.

For a long time, I held off because I couldn't decided what it was "supposed" to be.  Was it a series of how-tos on different .NET and AI topics?  Experiments that I was running but would never publish the results of because I would become bored and forget?  Or even some musings on the spiritual and other things going on in my life? 

I also held off because after being a content creator and streamer for years, being a blogger seemed like a step backwards.  Where content creators go when they don't know how to be relevant anymore or when they finally go corporate. 

Also, I held off because I had a note from late 2025 (at least that's the last time I updated it) with tons of ideas for blog posts.  And with how much AI has changed things, it almost feels like looking back into a time capsule from another time entirely!  Things I thought were such great ideas to write about, have either already happened, or are so far from the current state of things that they no longer make sense.

So, instead of a polished "publication," I've decided this is a _Digital Garden_.

## What is a Digital Garden?

Unlike a traditional blog where every post is a finished statement, a garden is a space where ideas are planted as seeds.

**Some things will grow:** Technical deep dives into AI synthesis or .NET workflows that I'll refine over time.

**Some things will be pruned:** Thoughts that don't "vibe" six months from now might just disappear or be archived.

**The species will be diverse:** You might see a post about orchestrating AI agents for a personal workout plan right next to a reflection on the "soul" of game design or musings on the deep questions of life.

## What to Expect (The Onslaught)

I'm currently in a phase of high-velocity experimentation. The "onslaught" of content coming your way will likely live at the intersection of a few messy, overlapping circles:

**The Lab:** I'm currently running an extended experiment using AI agents as a personal trainer. I'll be posting the raw data, the prompts, and the physical results (or failures) here.

**The Managerial Architecture:** Reflections on how AI is shifting the role of a Software Engineering Manager from a pure leader to a "system architect" of human-AI workflows.

**The Analog Escape:** Why, in a world of 2026-level digital abstraction, I still find it necessary to spend time with leather tools or brainstorming the "loop" of a simulation game.

And so many more things, too many to enumerate here. 

## A Disclaimer

This place is going to be confusing. It will be "all of the things" and "none of them" at the same time. If you're here for a specific niche, you might have to dodge a few stray thoughts on C# or tabletop gaming to find what you're looking for.

But that's the point of a garden. It's alive, it's a bit messy, and it's finally open for all to see.  

Hope you enjoy
